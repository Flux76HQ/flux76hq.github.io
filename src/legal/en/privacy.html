Flux76 builds software that keeps you in control of your data, and this website works the same way. This statement explains which personal data we process when you visit flux76.com or email us, why we do so, and what your rights are.

1. Who we are

Flux76 is an independent software studio in the Netherlands, registered with the Dutch Chamber of Commerce (KvK) under number 42123156. We are the controller of the data described in this statement.

Send questions about privacy to info@flux76.com.

2. What this statement covers

This statement covers the website flux76.com and contact with us by email. Our apps and services have their own privacy statements:

  • DiscVault: discvault.eu/privacy.html
  • Marktkraam.site: marktkraam.site/privacy (in Dutch)

If we work on a project for you, the agreement sets out who processes which data. Where that is required, we sign a data processing agreement.

3. What we do not do

  • We use no analytics and keep no visitor statistics.
  • We set no tracking cookies and show no advertising.
  • The site has no forms and no embedded social media posts. Fonts and images are served from flux76.com itself, so a visit does not reach other parties.
  • We do not sell data or pass it on to others for their own purposes.

The links to GitHub, Bluesky, X, Instagram and TikTok are plain links. Those services receive nothing from you until you click one, and then their own privacy policy applies.

4. When you visit the website

To show a page, your browser sends technical data such as your IP address, the time, the page requested and the type of browser. That data reaches the parties that deliver the site for us:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) hosts the website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure.
  • Cloudflare delivers the site quickly and protects it from abuse, such as attacks meant to take it offline.

We do not look at this data and do not use it to follow visitors. At most, Cloudflare can show us totals, such as the number of requests per day, that cannot be traced back to you. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in offering a secure, working website. GitHub and Cloudflare decide how long they keep this data.

5. Your choice of light or dark

If you use the button in the menu to choose a light or dark appearance, your browser remembers that choice under the name flux76_theme (in the browser's local storage). The choice stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. No consent is needed, because it only makes the site work the way you asked. You remove it by clearing the site data for flux76.com in your browser.

6. When you email us

If you email us about a project, with a question or for support with one of our apps, we process your name, your email address, the content of your message and anything else you choose to send. We use it to reply and, if it leads to a project, to prepare a proposal and an agreement.

  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering messages, or the steps needed before entering into an agreement with you.
  • Retention: for as long as the conversation runs and then for up to two years after the last message. If it leads to a project, we keep what belongs to our accounts for seven years, as Dutch law requires.
  • Email: our email runs on Microsoft 365. Microsoft processes the messages as our processor.

Please do not send us sensitive data, such as medical information or passwords, unless your question needs it.

7. Data outside the European Union

GitHub, Cloudflare and Microsoft are companies based in the United States. Where data leaves the European Economic Area as a result, this happens under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

8. Security

The website is only available over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). We take appropriate measures to protect the data you send us, and limit access to it to whoever needs it for your question or project.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the data we hold about you. You can also ask us to restrict processing, object to processing, and request your data in a common format to take elsewhere.

Email info@flux76.com to do so. We reply within one month. Sometimes we first ask you to confirm that the request really comes from you.

If you disagree with how we handle your data, please tell us. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

10. Changes

If the site or the way we process data changes, we update this statement. The date at the top shows when that last happened.
